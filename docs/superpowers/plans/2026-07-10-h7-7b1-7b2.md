### B2: H7 lane strategy (`strategies/h7_backtest.py`) — T→T+1 causality

`H7LaneBacktest(Strategy)`, parameters: lane, symbol, chain_provider, closes_adjusted (full series), closes_raw, earnings assertions + known_as_of policy hook, tradeable_assets, entry_cutoff, blocked_until.

Session S iteration order:
1. execute queued exits (queued at S-1 or scheduled for S); missing/one-sided leg quotes → pending-exit retained, retried next session; unresolved at expiration → RuntimeError (fail loud).
2. execute pending entry decided at S-1: revalidate (contract exists in S chain; DTE in band [F1]; earnings gate CLEAR; two-sided books; `passes_liquidity` both legs; executable cost/credit at S adverse quotes within budget and H7c credit floor). Valid → submit engine orders (fill at S pre-widened quotes); else cancel with exact reason into `cancelled_entries`.
3. observe exit triggers on S EOD (conservative liquidation marks from the S chain, haircut only — commissions never redefine thresholds):
   - long call TP: liquidation ≥ 2 × entry debit; spread TP: liquidation ≥ 0.75 × width; H7c TP: buyback ≤ 0.5 × credit; H7c stop: buyback ≥ 2 × credit; underlying stops vs adjusted close < stop_ref.
   - triggered → queue for S+1.
   - scheduled: queue when next session's DTE ≤ close threshold (H7_CLOSE_AT_DTE / H7C_CLOSE_AT_DTE) or next session is the last session before a known report (H7c; also gate-UNKNOWN [F2]) — executes ON the intended session.
   - multiple triggers: retain all reasons; primary priority `pre_earnings > scheduled_dte > underlying_stop > credit_stop > profit_target`.
4. compute new decision from S data when flat, warm (see B3), inside entry window: decide_lane_* on (adjusted closes ≤ S, S chain, raw spot at S, month_spent for symbol-lane-month, banned = gate != CLEAR) → store as pending entry for S+1.

Trade dicts extend the scoreboard shape with `lane, structure, exit_reasons, signal_date, underlying_entry_close, underlying_exit_close` (registered benchmark column).
