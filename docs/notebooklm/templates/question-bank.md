# NotebookLM Question Bank

Ask source-grounded questions like these, then verify important answers against
the uploaded source text before moving them into code or the ledger.

## Data

- What dates, symbols, and instruments does each source cover?
- What quote fields are present and how are they timestamped?
- Does the source document survivorship, delisting, or corporate-action handling?

## Options Mechanics

- How does the source treat early exercise and assignment?
- Are fees, bid/ask spreads, and slippage measured or assumed?
- Are fills modeled at mid, bid, ask, or another convention?

## Research Integrity

- Which facts are in-sample only?
- Which facts would touch the OOS window?
- Which claims require a pre-registered test before use?
- What would make the hypothesis invalid?
