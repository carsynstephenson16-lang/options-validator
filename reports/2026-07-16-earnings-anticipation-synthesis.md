## The honest causal map

1. **The print itself is mostly unforecastable from public data.** VST's four
   big print moves were driven by guidance revisions with no public
   precursor; VST is ~98–100% hedged in the current year, structurally
   disconnecting prints from the spot power prices people instinctively
   watch. The T-1 option surface already embeds every public signal in the
   landscape survey — so any registered test's null hypothesis must be
   "adds nothing beyond the chain's implied distribution."

2. **Genuine leads exist in only three places found, each with a specific
   clock:**
   - **TSMC monthly revenue 6-K → NVDA/AMD/AVGO** (~10th of each month,
     immutable SEC filing, 5–7 weeks before the fabless prints). Cleanest
     lead in the survey; free.
   - **Opportunistic insider clusters at T-45..T-15** (Cohen–Malloy–Pomorski
     lineage). The blackout rule means the informed window closes ~2 weeks
     before the print — look early, not late. EDGAR Form 4 is the strongest
     point-in-time source available (2-business-day lag, immutable).
   - **PJM capacity-auction results + the issuer's same-day 8-K** (VST
     2024-07-30: 10,255 MW @ $273.45/MW-day; 2025-07-22: ~10,314 MW @
     $329.17): scheduled, quantified, 9–16 days before the next print,
     11–23 months before revenue recognition. Caveat: the stock absorbs most
     of it on auction day (+14.8% / +5.8%), n=2, both up-auctions.

3. **The inversion is the actionable insight for the options program:** for
   VST/CEG the real vol events are the **scheduled auction dates and
   unscheduled deal 8-Ks, not the prints** — VST's same-day print reaction
   shrank from ≥6.9% (2024) to ≤4% (last five). Pre-earnings long-vol
   structures on VST are aimed at the wrong calendar square. PJM auction
   dates belong in the same catalyst class as FOMC/earnings for any
   catalyst-aware window or dashboard.

4. **Disqualified, verified not assumed:** congressional STOCK Act trades
   (45-day disclosure lag arrives after the print; ~11 VST/CEG trades total),
   EIA generation data (2-month lag), spot/forward power prices (hedge
   book), VST insider Form 4s in-sample (calendar grants + CEO 10b5-1, not
   opportunistic), PPA announcements as print predictors (the announcement
   IS the surprise; direction inconsistent: TMI +22%, Meta ~0%, VST/AWS
   −4.5%). Also: free point-in-time consensus history does not exist —
   forward snapshot collection is the only clean path, and it starts only
   when someone starts it.

5. **The statistics constraint that must be faced at registration, not
   after:** on a 14-name universe the promising events are rare (~12 TSMC
   read-through events/yr; fewer insider clusters). Against an
   option-surface null, reaching significance takes years. Design choices
   that respect this: pooled-event designs, pre-registered one-run studies
   on history (H9-style), and honest INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE vocabularies.

6. **Timeline-ledger tie-in (equity-research book):** the ranked proposals
   are (i) zero-risk catalyst annotations on rendered windows (now including
   PJM auction dates for power names), and (ii) an earnings-jump **variance**
   component sized from the chain's implied move as a pre-registered
   challenger — variance, not drift, because directional post-earnings drift
   has decayed to near-zero in large caps. Market-drift terms: park; the
   frozen design's own anti-thesis-restatement argument survives scrutiny.

7. **Point-in-time hazards found first-hand:** USASpending restated a
   Palantir award figure ~4 months after the fact (a naive contract tracker
   would backfill hindsight); EDGAR daily-index files are filing-date-keyed
   (verified with live Form 4 rows) and are the correct spine for
   reconstruction; job postings are ephemeral without proactive archiving.
   One open data conflict: VST 2026-02-26 close-to-close computed +0.8% vs
   press "≥5% slide" — UNVERIFIED, needs a second canonical close source.
